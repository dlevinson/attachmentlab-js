Test current realized-network access as a mechanism in both free and mesh families.

## Settings

## Reading guide

- Current accessibility semantics are realized-network semantics. Compare arrival-only, target-only, and both against no-access in free and mesh families.
- If access weighting raises gravity but also increases inequality or directional concentration, it is acting more like centrality reinforcement than exogenous opportunity access.
