# sensitivity findings

- Within the general-model sensitivity set, the shortest mean links occur in phi_5 (0.094), while the longest occur in phi_0 (0.522). This identifies cost deterrence as the strongest edge-length lever in the baseline model.
- K_4 shows the highest saturation share (100.0%), indicating that the strongest binding pressure in this tranche comes from the capacity block rather than from scale alone.
- K_4 has the highest mean clustering (1.000), which supports the expectation that higher attachment multiplicity rather than pure preferential attachment is the main local-cycle driver.
- Relative to the baseline scenario, K=4 and beta=2 should be read mainly as saturation/truncation mechanisms, while phi sweeps should be read mainly as link-length mechanisms.
